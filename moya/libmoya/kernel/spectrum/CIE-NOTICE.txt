moya carries three data tables of the International Commission on Illumination (CIE), in
moya/libmoya/kernel/spectrum/CieObserver.h, moya/libmoya/kernel/spectrum/CieD65.h and
moya/libmoya/kernel/spectrum/CieDaylight.h:

  CIE 2019, Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard colorimetric observer,
  International Commission on Illumination (CIE), Vienna, AT, DOI: 10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b

  CIE 2019, CIE standard illuminant D65, International Commission on Illumination (CIE),
  Vienna, AT, DOI: 10.25039/CIE.DS.hjfjmt59

  CIE 2018, Components of the relative spectral distribution of daylight used in the
  calculation of relative spectral power distributions of CIE daylight illuminants of different
  correlated colour temperatures, International Commission on Illumination (CIE), Vienna, AT,
  DOI: 10.25039/CIE.DS.w7zunnny

Copyright (c) CIE. The tables are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International licence, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/, and so are the three
files that hold them. Their values are written as C++ float literals, which rounds each to single
precision. No value is otherwise changed.
